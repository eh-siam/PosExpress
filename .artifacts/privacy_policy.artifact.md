# Privacy Policy for POS Express

**Last Updated:** September 01, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **POS Express** ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Information:** When you sign up or log in via Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture.
- **Business Data:** Information about your products, categories, and sales transactions.
- **Customer Information:** If you input customer names or phone numbers during a transaction, this data is stored in your account's database.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information:** We may collect info about your mobile device (model, OS version).
- **Usage Data:** Through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, we collect data on how the app is used and reports on any crashes to improve performance.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain the POS service.
- To sync your data across devices using Firebase Realtime Database.
- To process transactions and generate receipts.
- To monitor app performance and fix bugs.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Your data is stored securely using **Google Firebase**. We do not sell your personal or business data to third parties. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

## 4. Permissions
- **Camera:** Used solely for scanning QR codes to identify products.
- **Internet:** Required for Firebase data syncing and authentication.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- Google Play Services
- Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Analytics, Crashlytics)

## 6. Your Rights
You can delete your account and data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us.
